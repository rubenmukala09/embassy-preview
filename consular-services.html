Service 01

Passports

Issuance and renewal of biometric passports of the Democratic Republic of the Congo for citizens residing in the United States, including first-time issuance, renewal of expired passports and replacement of lost or damaged passports.

Who: DRC citizens in the U.S. Processing: ≈ 90 days Fee: US $75

How to apply - three steps

  1. Pre-register & pay online. Obtain a Tax ID (NIF) at the e‑NIF portal ↗, complete your application on the national passport portal passeport.gouv.cd ↗, and pay US $75 via the Equity BCDC link. Allow 72 hours for the system to register your payment.
  2. Email your documents to consular@ambardcusa.org:
    • Biometric Information Sheet
    • Notice of Assessment (Note de Perception) + Equity BCDC proof of payment
    • Copy of your current or previous passport (bio-data page only)
  3. Attend your biometric appointment with the printed sheets, your current passport (a police report if lost or stolen), proof of Congolese nationality, a valid U.S. residency document (Green Card, work permit or I‑20) and a pre-paid, tracked return envelope. Minors also need an original birth certificate, legalized parental authorization and a parent’s passport.

Average processing time is about 90 days, as passports are produced in Kinshasa. Until further notice, the Embassy is unable to issue passports to individuals holding refugee status.

Service 02

Visas

Entry visas for travel to the Democratic Republic of the Congo, available to U.S. citizens and foreign nationals legally residing in the United States.

Tourist Business Transit
Processing: 5–10 business days Fee: from $120

Requirements checklist

✓Passport valid for at least 6 months
✓Completed visa application form
✓One passport-size photograph
✓Proof of accommodation or letter of invitation
✓Yellow fever vaccination certificate

Service 03

Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer)

A temporary travel document (Laissez-Passer) issued to DR Congolese citizens who need to travel to the DRC when a valid passport is not available, for example in cases of loss, expiry or urgent return.

Purpose: emergency / one-way travel Processing: 2–3 business days Fee: $80

Requirements checklist

✓Completed Laissez-Passer request form
✓Proof of DRC nationality
✓Two passport-size photographs
✓Police report (for lost or stolen passport)
✓Proof of travel (ticket or itinerary)

Service 04

Legalization of Documents

Authentication and legalization of documents, civil records, academic diplomas, powers of attorney and commercial papers, so they can be recognized and used officially in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Purpose: official use in the DRC Processing: 3–5 business days Fee: $50 per document

Requirements checklist

✓Original document to be legalized
✓One photocopy of the document
✓Prior notarization or apostille where required
✓Copy of applicant's identification
✓Payment of the per-document fee

Help & guidance

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about our consular services.

Biometric passports take on average about 90 days, as they are produced in Kinshasa. Visas are processed in a minimum of 1–2 business days once a complete application is received; Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer) and document legalization are handled within a few business days. Times begin once a complete application and payment have been received.
Consular fees are payable by money order or certified cashier's check made out to the "Embassy of the DRC." Personal checks and cash sent by mail are not accepted. The exact, non-refundable fee for your service must accompany your application.
Yes. Mail-in applications are accepted for most services. Include your completed form, all required documents, the applicable fee by money order, and a prepaid, self-addressed return envelope with tracking. We recommend using a trackable courier service for your records.
There are no online bookings at this time. Consular applications are received in person during consular hours, Monday to Friday, 10:00–13:00; bring your completed forms, original documents and payment by money order. Applications may also be submitted by mail with a prepaid, trackable return envelope.
Report the loss or theft to your local police and obtain a police report. Then apply for a replacement passport or, if you must travel urgently, a Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer). Bring the police report, proof of DRC nationality, and two passport-size photographs.
The Embassy does not generally provide same-day service. Emergency travel documents such as the Tenant-Lieu may be expedited in genuine cases of urgency, for example a death in the family or medical emergency, subject to supporting documentation and Embassy approval.
Nationality rules are governed by the laws of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and may change. For your individual situation, please contact the consular section directly so we can advise you based on your specific circumstances and current regulations.
