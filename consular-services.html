Service 01

Passports

Issuance and renewal of biometric passports of the Democratic Republic of the Congo for citizens residing in the United States, including first-time issuance, renewal of expired passports and replacement of lost or damaged passports.

Who: DRC citizens in the U.S. Processing: ≈ 90 days Fee: US $75

How to apply - three steps

  1. Pre-register & pay online. Obtain a Tax ID (NIF) at the current DGI-linked e‑NIF portal ↗, complete your application on the national passport portal passeport.gouv.cd ↗, and pay US $75 through the approved passport process. Allow 72 hours for the system to register your payment.
  2. Email your documents to ambassade@ambardcusa.org:
    • Biometric Information Sheet
    • Notice of Assessment (Note de Perception) + Equity BCDC proof of payment
    • Copy of your current or previous passport (bio-data page only)
  3. Attend your biometric appointment with the printed sheets, your current passport (a police report if lost or stolen), proof of Congolese nationality, a valid U.S. residency document (Green Card, work permit or I‑20) and a pre-paid, tracked return envelope. Minors also need an original birth certificate, legalized parental authorization and a parent’s passport.

Average processing time is about 90 days, as passports are produced in Kinshasa. Until further notice, the Embassy is unable to issue passports to individuals holding refugee status.

Service 02 · Official requirements

Visas

Entry visas for travel to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The Embassy requires a complete, signed paper application and a passport with at least six months of remaining validity and two adjacent blank visa pages.

TourismBusinessStudyTransitDiplomatic & official
Published processing guidance
The Embassy states a minimum of one to two business days after a complete file is received. Applications are not processed more than three months before the planned travel date. Visa fees are non-refundable, and issuance, validity and number of entries remain at the discretion of DRC immigration services.
Core checklist for most applicants
  • One application form, completed in full, dated and signed
  • One recent 2 × 2 inch passport photograph
  • Yellow-fever vaccination card copy and flight itinerary copy
  • Passport bio-page copy and previous DRC visa copies, if applicable
  • Original signed passport with at least six months’ validity and two adjacent blank visa pages
  • Prepaid, tracked return envelope such as FedEx or UPS
  • Payment by the method published in the fee section; use paper clips or tape rather than staples
U.S. citizens · two-year reciprocity visa

The Embassy publishes a two-year, US $185 reciprocity visa for U.S. citizens travelling for tourism, study or business. The required supporting documents still depend on the purpose of travel.

Tourist and business supporting documents
  • Tourism: a notarized invitation from a tourism agency or private host; if travelling independently, a notarized purpose letter, planned sites, hotel reservation, itinerary and evidence of sufficient funds
  • Business: an original company letter explaining the trip and a signed invitation from the DRC company or partners, including the local contact’s name, address and telephone number
  • Applicants of Congolese origin: the Embassy states that a host invitation is not required
Children under 10
  • Application form completed and signed by a parent
  • Copy of the child’s birth certificate
  • Copy of the parent’s passport
  • Parental authorization
Non-U.S. citizens and diplomatic or official travel
  • Non-U.S. citizens: include a Permanent Resident Card or U.S. visa and I-94 record
  • Diplomatic or official files: include the official U.S. Department of State letter describing the purpose of travel
  • Diplomatic and U.S. official visa fees are published as waived

Service 03 · One-way travel document

Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer)

The Embassy may issue this temporary document to a Congolese national who cannot obtain a replacement passport in time. It authorizes one-way travel to the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Congolese nationalsOne-way to the DRCTracked return required

Published requirements

  • Completed Tenant-Lieu de Passeport application form
  • Copy of the current or previous Congolese passport; include a police report if lost or stolen
  • Two recent 2 × 2 inch passport photographs
  • Proof of valid U.S. residence, such as a Green Card, I-20, work permit or employment authorization
  • Prepaid return label or tracked return envelope
  • Payment using the current Embassy-approved method

Service 04 · Civil and notarial affairs

Legalization of Documents

The Embassy publishes civil-affairs and notarial services for documents connected to birth, marriage, inheritance, death, powers of attorney and other official purposes.

Birth recordsMarriage recordsInheritanceNotarial servicesPowers of attorney

Document-specific guidance

  • Birth certificate: signed form and a passport or other evidence that the person was born in the DRC
  • Marriage certificate: passport copies for both spouses and the marriage record established in the DRC
  • Inheritance certificate: passport copy and the certificate established in the DRC
  • Notarial work: confirm originals, identity documents and signatures required for the specific transaction
Confirm before mailing originals. Requirements differ by document type. Use the published fee table below and contact the Embassy before sending irreplaceable civil-status or notarial documents.

Help & guidance

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about our consular services.

Biometric passports take on average about 90 days, as they are produced in Kinshasa. Visas are processed in a minimum of 1–2 business days once a complete application is received; Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer) and document legalization are handled within a few business days. Times begin once a complete application and payment have been received.
The Embassy's current consular guidance instructs applicants to use a blank money order only. Fees are non-refundable. Confirm the exact amount for your service before submitting the file.
Yes. Mail-in applications are accepted for most services. Include your completed form, all required documents, the applicable fee by money order, and a prepaid, self-addressed return envelope with tracking. We recommend using a trackable courier service for your records.
Visa drop-off is published for Monday–Thursday, 10:00–15:00, and Friday, 10:00–13:00. Passport biometric appointments follow online pre-registration and Embassy confirmation. Confirm the correct pathway for your service before travelling.
Report the loss or theft to your local police and obtain a police report. Then apply for a replacement passport or, if you must travel urgently, a Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer). Bring the police report, proof of DRC nationality, and two passport-size photographs.
The Embassy does not generally provide same-day service. Emergency travel documents such as the Tenant-Lieu may be expedited in genuine cases of urgency, for example a death in the family or medical emergency, subject to supporting documentation and Embassy approval.
Nationality rules are governed by the laws of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and may change. For your individual situation, please contact the consular section directly so we can advise you based on your specific circumstances and current regulations.
