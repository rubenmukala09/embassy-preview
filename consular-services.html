Travel documents

Passports

Issuance and renewal of biometric passports of the Democratic Republic of the Congo for citizens residing in the United States, including first-time issuance, renewal of expired passports and replacement of lost or damaged passports.

At a glance

Who can apply
Congolese citizens residing in the United States. Until further notice, the Embassy is unable to issue passports to individuals holding refugee status.
Fee
US $75, paid online through the Equity BCDC link emailed after pre-registration.
Processing time
About 90 days on average, as passports are produced in Kinshasa.
How to submit
Pre-register online, then email your documents to the Embassy.
Where
In person at the Embassy for biometrics, once the Embassy confirms your appointment by email.

How to apply, step by step

These steps follow the Embassy’s biometric passport communiqué of 5 August 2025. Confirm current hours, contacts and payment instructions with the Embassy before you begin.

  1. Get your tax identification number (NIF)

    Obtain your NIF through the DGI e‑NIF service: e-nif.dgirdc.cd

  2. Pre-register and pay US $75

    Complete your passport pre-registration on the national passport portal: passeport.gouv.cd

    Pay the published US $75 passport fee through the Equity BCDC link emailed after pre-registration. Allow 72 hours for the payment to be recorded.

  3. Email four documents to the Embassy

    After completing the online formalities, email these four items to the Embassy:

    • the biometric information sheet obtained after pre-registration
    • the Note de Perception (notice of assessment)
    • proof of payment through Equity BCDC
    • a copy of your current or previous passport (bio-data page only)

    ambassade@ambardcusa.org

  4. Attend your appointment with the originals

    Wait for the Embassy’s appointment-confirmation email, then come to the Embassy in person with:

    • the documents you emailed in step 3
    • your current passport, whether valid, full or expired (a police report if it was lost or stolen)
    • proof of Congolese nationality, such as a nationality certificate or birth certificate
    • a valid U.S. residence document, such as a Green Card, valid work permit or student I-20
    • a pre-paid, tracked return envelope

  5. If the applicant is a child under 18

    For a child’s appointment, also bring:

    • the child’s original birth certificate
    • a legalized parental authorization
    • the child’s original Green Card
    • the child’s passport, whether valid or expired
    • the Congolese parent’s passport, or another document requested by the Embassy

  6. Collect your passport

    Passports are produced in Kinshasa and take about 90 days on average. Once your passport is back at the Embassy, it is available for collection or for return delivery in your tracked envelope. Keep your official receipt or reference to follow up.

Start pre-registration on passeport.gouv.cd

Entry to the DRC

Visas

Entry visas for travel to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The Embassy requires a complete, signed paper application and a passport with at least six months of remaining validity and two adjacent blank visa pages.

At a glance

Processing time
A minimum of one to two business days after a complete file is received. Applications are not processed more than three months before the planned travel date.
Fees
From US $45 (airport transit, one way) to US $450 (multiple entry, 6 months). Visa fees are non-refundable. See the fee schedule
Drop-off
Monday to Thursday, 10:00–15:00; Friday, 10:00–13:00
Pick-up
Tuesday and Thursday, 14:00–15:30; Friday, 11:00–12:30
Decision
Issuance, validity and number of entries remain at the discretion of DRC immigration services.

What to include

Core checklist for most applicants
  • One application form, completed in full, dated and signed
  • One recent 2 × 2 inch passport photograph
  • A copy of your yellow-fever vaccination card and a copy of your flight itinerary
  • A copy of the passport bio-page and of any previous DRC visas
  • Your original signed passport, with at least six months’ validity and two adjacent blank visa pages
  • A prepaid, tracked return envelope, such as FedEx or UPS
  • Payment by the method published in the fee schedule; use paper clips or tape rather than staples
U.S. citizens: two-year reciprocity visa

The Embassy publishes a two-year, US $185 reciprocity visa for U.S. citizens travelling for tourism, study or business. The supporting documents still depend on the purpose of travel.

Tourist and business supporting documents
  • Tourism: a notarized invitation from a tourism agency or private host; if travelling independently, a notarized letter stating the purpose of travel, planned sites, hotel reservation, itinerary and evidence of sufficient funds
  • Business: an original company letter explaining the trip and a signed invitation from the DRC company or partners, including the local contact’s name, address and telephone number
  • Applicants of Congolese origin: the Embassy states that a host invitation is not required
Children under 10
  • Application form completed and signed by a parent
  • Copy of the child’s birth certificate
  • Copy of the parent’s passport
  • Parental authorization
Non-U.S. citizens and diplomatic or official travel
  • Non-U.S. citizens: include a Permanent Resident Card or U.S. visa and I-94 record
  • Diplomatic or official files: include the official U.S. Department of State letter describing the purpose of travel
  • Diplomatic and U.S. official visa fees are published as waived

One-way travel document

Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer)

The Embassy may issue this temporary document to a Congolese national who cannot obtain a replacement passport in time. It authorizes one-way travel to the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

What to send

  • The completed Tenant-Lieu de Passeport application form
  • A copy of the current or previous Congolese passport, with a police report if it was lost or stolen
  • Two recent 2 × 2 inch passport photographs
  • Proof of valid U.S. residence, such as a Green Card, I-20, work permit or employment authorization
  • A prepaid return label or tracked return envelope
  • Payment using the current Embassy-approved method

Civil status and notarial

Legalization of documents

Civil-status and notarial services for documents connected to birth, marriage, inheritance, death, powers of attorney and other official purposes.

What each document needs

Birth certificate
A signed form and a passport or other evidence that the person was born in the DRC
Marriage certificate
Passport copies for both spouses and the marriage record established in the DRC
Inheritance certificate
A passport copy and the certificate established in the DRC
Notarial work
Confirm the originals, identity documents and signatures required for the specific transaction

Requirements differ by document type. Contact the Embassy before mailing irreplaceable civil-status or notarial originals.

After you apply

Application status

Online tracking is not available. Keep your official receipt or reference and contact the Consular Section for current assistance.

What happens after you apply

For documents produced in Kinshasa, such as passports:

  1. SubmittedReceived and receipted at the consular counter
  2. In transitDispatched to Kinshasa, where central issuance applies
  3. ProcessingReviewed and produced by the issuing authority
  4. ReturnedBack at the Embassy in Washington, D.C.

Help and guidance

Common questions

Can I apply by mail?

Not for a passport: after emailing your documents, you attend the Embassy in person for biometrics. Visa and Tenant-Lieu files include a prepaid, tracked return envelope or label. Before posting original documents, confirm with the Embassy that your service can be handled by mail.

How do I book an appointment?

There is no online booking. For passports, the Embassy confirms your biometric appointment by email after receiving your documents. Visa files are dropped off during the visa drop-off hours. For other services, contact the Embassy before travelling.

What if my passport is lost or stolen?

Report the loss or theft to your local police and obtain a police report. Then apply for a replacement passport or, if you must travel urgently, a Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer). Bring the police report, proof of DRC nationality, and two passport-size photographs.

Do you offer same-day service?

The Embassy does not publish a same-day service. If you must travel urgently and cannot obtain a replacement passport in time, ask the Embassy about a Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer), which authorizes one-way travel to the DRC.

Can I hold dual nationality?

Nationality rules are governed by the laws of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and may change. For your individual situation, contact the Consular Section directly for advice based on your circumstances and current regulations.
