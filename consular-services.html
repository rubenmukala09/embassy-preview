The Consular Affairs division of the Embassy provides consular services to Congolese nationals living in the United States, issues visas to U.S. citizens and to any foreign national who wishes to visit the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and legalizes official documents.

Travel documents

Passports

Biometric passports of the Democratic Republic of the Congo for Congolese nationals living in the United States: first issuance, renewal and replacement. All passport applications must now be initiated online.

At a glance

Who issues passports
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Kinshasa issues all passports. The Embassy collects your biometric data and forwards it to the Ministry for processing.
Who can apply
Congolese nationals residing in the United States, with a valid U.S. residency document.
Refugee and asylum status
Until further notice, the Embassy is unable to issue passports to individuals holding refugee status.
Fee
USD 75, paid online through the Equity BCDC link emailed after pre-registration. The total may vary with bank charges: the base amount is USD 75, plus any additional fees shown during the process.
Processing time
90 days on average. The Embassy cannot give a more precise timeline, as production depends on Kinshasa.
Questions
Call +1 (202) 234-7691 or +1 (202) 507-4611, or email ambassade@ambardcusa.org

How to apply, in three steps

  1. Pre-register and pay online

    Before you visit the Embassy, complete these online formalities:

    1. Tax identification number (NIF): obtain your NIF from the Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) through its e‑NIF service: e-nif.dgirdc.cd
    2. Online pre-registration: complete the application form on the official passport website: passeport.gouv.cd
    3. Payment: pay the USD 75 fee through the Equity BCDC link sent to you by email after pre-registration, and check your spam folder for it. The total may vary with bank charges and any additional fees shown during the process.

    Wait 72 hours after payment for your transaction to be fully registered in the system.

  2. Email your documents to request an appointment

    Once the online steps are complete, email these documents to the Embassy:

    • the biometric information sheet, obtained after pre-registration
    • the notice of assessment (Note de Perception) and the Equity BCDC proof of payment
    • a copy of your current or previous Congolese passport (bio page only)

    ambassade@ambardcusa.org

    This is the passport address given in the Embassy’s instructions. Note the spelling “ambardcusa.org”: it differs from the general address info@ambadrcusa.org.

  3. Attend your appointment for biometric capture

    When you have received the appointment confirmation by email, come to the Embassy in person with these original documents:

    • the printed biometric sheet, notice of assessment and proof of payment
    • your current passport, whether valid, full or expired; if it was lost or stolen, a police report or your birth certificate
    • proof of Congolese nationality, such as a nationality certificate or birth certificate
    • a valid U.S. residency document, such as a Green Card, work permit or, for students, an I-20
    • a prepaid return mailing envelope (FedEx, UPS or USPS) with a tracking number, so that the Embassy can mail your passport to you once it is ready

  4. If the applicant is a minor

    For a child’s appointment, bring these originals:

    • the child’s birth certificate and a legalized parental authorization
    • the child’s Green Card and previous passport, if applicable
    • the Congolese parent’s passport, or other necessary identification

After your appointment, allow about 90 days: the Embassy mails your passport in your prepaid envelope once it is ready.

Entry to the DRC

Visas

Every traveler needs a visa application form, printed, completed and signed in blue or black ink, and a valid passport to enter the Democratic Republic of the Congo. All requirements must be met for the DRC Government to process your application.

At a glance

Processing time
A minimum of one to two business days after a complete application is received. Processing only starts once all documents are received.
Diplomatic and official
Requests from foreign officials can be processed on the same day or within 24 hours. For same-day service, allow 2 hours.
When to apply
No more than three months before your travel dates. Requests received earlier are not processed.
Fees
From $45 (airport transit, one way) to $450 (multiple entries, 6 months); $185 for the two-year visa for U.S. citizens. Visa fees are non-refundable. Visa fees
Drop-off
Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m.–⁠1:00 p.m.
Pick-up
Monday to Thursday, 2:00–⁠4:00 p.m.; Friday, 10:00 a.m.–⁠1:00 p.m.
Decision
Every application is reviewed by the DRC immigration services, which decide on issuance, duration and number of entries.

Documents for your category

Open the category that applies to you, then check the additional requirements below it.

Congolese nationals (diaspora)

For applicants of Congolese origin:

  1. One visa application form, completed in full, dated and signed by the applicant.
  2. One passport-style photograph (2 × 2 in) taken within the last 6 months.
  3. Payment by money order, cashier’s check or certified check addressed to the Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (see the payment note).
  4. A copy of your yellow fever vaccination card.
  5. A copy of your flight itinerary (not an actual booking or ticket).
  6. A valid, signed passport with at least two blank pages marked “Visas” side by side and at least six months of validity remaining.
  7. A prepaid mailing envelope for the return of your passport, using a service with tracking such as FedEx or UPS.

Processing starts only once all documents are received and takes at least one business day.

Diplomatic and official

Requests from foreign officials can be processed on the same day or within 24 hours. For same-day service, allow 2 hours for processing. Fees are waived for diplomatic and U.S. official visas.

  1. One visa application form, completed in full, dated and signed by the applicant.
  2. One passport-style photograph (2 × 2 in) taken within the last 6 months.
  3. An official letter from the U.S. Department of State stating the purpose of the trip.
  4. A copy of your yellow fever vaccination card.
  5. A copy of your flight itinerary (not an actual booking or ticket).
  6. A copy of the passport ID page, and copies of any previous DRC visas.
  7. A valid, signed passport with at least two blank pages marked “Visas” side by side and at least six months of validity remaining.
  8. A prepaid mailing envelope for the return of the passport, using a service with tracking such as FedEx or UPS.

Processing starts only once all documents are received and requires at least one business day, unless same-day or 24-hour service is arranged as above.

All other travelers

Including applicants of Congolese origin. Individuals of Congolese descent are not required to present an invitation.

  1. One visa application form, completed in full, dated and signed by the applicant.
  2. One passport-style photograph (2 × 2 in) taken within the last 6 months.
  3. Payment by money order, cashier’s check or certified check addressed to the Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (see the payment note).
  4. A notarized invitation letter from your host.
  5. A company letter explaining the purpose of the trip (business travelers only).
  6. A copy of your yellow fever vaccination card.
  7. A copy of your flight itinerary (not an actual booking or ticket).
  8. A copy of your passport (bio page only) and copies of any previous DRC visas.
  9. A valid, signed passport with at least two blank pages marked “Visas” side by side and at least six months of validity remaining.
  10. A prepaid mailing envelope for the return of your passport, using a service with tracking such as FedEx or UPS.

Processing starts only once all documents are received and requires at least two business days.

Children under 10

For a child under the age of 10, these four documents must be provided for a visa to be issued:

  1. The visa application form, duly completed and signed by one of the parents.
  2. A copy of the birth certificate.
  3. A copy of the parents’ passport.
  4. A parental authorization.
U.S. citizens: two-year visa, $185

The DRC and the United States have signed an agreement to extend visa reciprocity to Congolese and American citizens traveling for tourism, study and business. The visa is valid for 2 years and costs $185. U.S. citizens submit the documents listed for their travel category above.

Additional requirements: check the ones that apply to you

Non-U.S. citizens
Also include a copy of your Permanent Resident Card, or of your U.S. visa and I-94. If you entered the United States after April 26, 2013, print the electronic I-94 form from i94.cbp.dhs.gov.
Business travelers
Also include a letter of invitation from the company or partners you will visit in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, on company letterhead and signed by a company representative. It should give your name and the name, address and telephone number of a contact person at the company overseas. The company letter must bear the original pen-to-paper signature of a company representative other than the applicant.
Tourist travelers
Also include a notarized letter of invitation from a tourism agency or private host in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. If you are not using a tourism agency or a private host, provide a letter stating the purpose of the trip, the sites you plan to visit, your hotel reservation and the duration of the trip, notarized by local authorities in the United States, together with proof of sufficient funds for the trip, a travel itinerary and your hotel reservation.

Before you send your application

  • For safety reasons, use paper clips or tape instead of staples.
  • Avoid submitting multiple applications and photos: only one of each is needed.
  • Missing documents will delay processing or cause your application to be returned.
  • If you represent an agency, make sure your clients meet all the requirements above before submitting their applications to the Embassy.

All applications submitted to the Embassy are reviewed by the DRC immigration services, which reserve the right to issue or deny a visa to any applicant. The issuance of visas, including visa duration and number of entries, is at their discretion.

One-way travel document

Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer)

If you cannot get a replacement passport in time to travel to the DRC, the Embassy may be able to provide a travel document. This travel document, the Tenant-Lieu, allows Congolese nationals to travel one way to the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Looking for a birth certificate? It is handled with Legalization and civil affairs.

What to send

  1. The completed “Tenant-Lieu de Passeport” application form.
  2. A copy of your current or previous Congolese passport. The passport cannot be more than 6 months past its expiry date.If your passport was lost or stolen, provide a written police report.
  3. Two passport-style photographs (2 × 2 in) taken within the last 6 months.
  4. A copy of your proof of valid residence: Green Card, study visa or I-20, work permit or employment authorization.
  5. A prepaid return shipping label, or a prepaid return envelope with stamps and a tracking number, to return your document when it is ready.
  6. Payment by money order, cashier’s check or certified check addressed to the Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (see the payment note).
Fee
Not listed in the Embassy’s published fee schedule. Confirm the amount with the Embassy before you pay.
How to submit
Mail your application to the Embassy or drop it off at the Embassy.

Repatriating the remains of a Congolese national

For the consulate to authorize the repatriation to the DRC of the remains of a Congolese national who has died in the United States, the Embassy requires:

  1. Proof that the deceased person is a Congolese citizen, such as a passport copy or birth certificate.
  2. A certified copy of the death certificate.
  3. A letter from a local health authority certifying that the deceased did not die of a contagious disease.
  4. A metal, hermetically sealable casket.
  5. A letter stating how the body was embalmed, typically signed by the director of a funeral home or a counterpart at another institution.
  6. A self-addressed, prepaid envelope (A4 size) for the return of your documents, using a service with tracking such as FedEx or UPS. USPS, metered stamps and regular mail are not acceptable.

The permit costs $150 when the deceased does not have a valid Congolese passport and a Tenant-Lieu has to be created. If the passport is valid, the permit costs $100.

Help and guidance

Common questions

Can I apply by mail?

Visa and Tenant-Lieu applications can be mailed to the Embassy or dropped off in person, with a prepaid, tracked return envelope or label. Passports cannot: after pre-registering online and emailing your documents, you attend the Embassy in person for biometric capture. Before posting original civil-status or notarial documents, check with the Embassy.

How do I book an appointment?

Until further notice, there are no online bookings. For passports, the Embassy confirms your biometric appointment by email after receiving your documents. Visa applications are dropped off during the visa drop-off hours. For other services, contact the Embassy before you travel.

What if my passport is lost or stolen?

Report it to your local police and get a written police report. To replace it, follow the passport steps and bring the police report or your birth certificate in place of the passport at your appointment (step 3). If you must travel before a new passport arrives, apply for a Tenant-Lieu, which needs the police report and has its own list of documents.

Do you offer same-day service?

Only for diplomatic and official visa requests, which can be processed on the same day (allow 2 hours) or within 24 hours. Other visas take at least one to two business days after a complete application is received, and passports about 90 days. If you must travel urgently and cannot get a replacement passport in time, ask the Embassy about a Tenant-Lieu (Laissez-Passer), which allows one-way travel to the DRC.

How early can I apply for a visa?

No more than three months before your travel dates: the Embassy does not process visa requests received earlier than that.

Can I hold dual nationality?

Nationality rules are governed by the laws of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and may change. For your individual situation, contact the Consular Section directly for advice based on your circumstances and current regulations.
