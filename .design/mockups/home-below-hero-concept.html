Welcome

Information for visitors, investors and the Congolese diaspora

Welcome to the official website of the Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) in the United States. We extend a warm invitation to explore all the essential information for your visit to our beautiful country.

The DRC boasts the world's second-largest river basin and is home to five natural UNESCO World Heritage sites: four national parks and the Okapi Wildlife Reserve. Whether you are a tourist seeking adventure or an investor looking to engage with the DRC, we believe your visit will contribute significantly to our country's vision.

The Embassy

The Head of State and the Ambassador

The Embassy advances the interests of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and its citizens, and strengthens cooperation, peace, prosperity and sustainable development with the United States.

Head of State

H.E. Félix-Antoine Tshisekedi Tshilombo

President of the Democratic Republic of the Congo

In office since January 24, 2019. The Embassy carries out its mission under the authority of the President of the Republic.
