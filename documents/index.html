Attach a document

Send us your file

Enter the reference number from your appointment or application confirmation, choose the document type, and upload the file. You can add more than one - upload them one at a time.

Your file is stored on a private disk and only released to you (with your reference and last name) or the reviewing officer.

What to bring

✓Your consular reference number (from your confirmation email)
✓Clear, full-page scans - all four corners visible
✓Passport application: form, current passport bio page, and photo
✓Visa: invitation or purpose letter and supporting identity documents
✓Civil records: birth, marriage or death certificate scans
