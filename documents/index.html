Document submission guidance

Prepare supporting documents for your consular request. Online upload and tracking are not connected in this preview.

Use this guide to prepare the supporting documents for a passport, visa, Tenant‑Lieu or civil-status request before you visit the Embassy or send your file by post.

Prototype service. Online upload and tracking are not available on this website, and until further notice the Embassy takes no online bookings. Email documents only where the Embassy’s instructions ask you to: for passports, step 2 asks you to email your documents to ambassade@ambardcusa.org.

Checklists

Before you start

Who this is for
Applicants for a passport, visa, Tenant‑Lieu, or a civil-status or notarial document
How to submit
Passports: online pre-registration, documents by email, then in person for biometric capture. Visas and Tenant-Lieu: in person or by post.
Processing time
Visas: at least 1–2 business days after a complete file is received; diplomatic and official requests can be processed the same day (allow 2 hours) or within 24 hours. Visa requests are not processed more than three months before travel. Biometric passports: 90 days on average, as they are produced in Kinshasa.
Fees
Money order or check, depending on the service (the Embassy’s published instructions differ, so confirm the method first); passport fees are paid online. No refund will be granted. Fees and payment guidance
Online bookings
None until further notice.

Step by step

Prepare your documents

  1. Check the requirements for your service

    Requirements differ by service and can change. Use the checklist for your service further down this page, and confirm it against the current consular guidance.

    Go to the document checklist

  2. Complete and sign the application form

    Use the current form for your service. Print it, complete it in full, then date and sign it in blue or black ink. Send one form and one photo only.

    Forms and downloads

  3. Gather originals and copies

    Bring each original document with one photocopy. Copies must be clear, full-page and show all four corners.

  4. Add the fee and a return envelope

    Include the exact fee by the method listed for your service (passport fees are paid online before you apply) and a prepaid, tracked return envelope such as FedEx or UPS. For safety reasons, use paper clips or tape rather than staples.

    Payment guidance

  5. Submit your file and keep your receipt

    Hand in your file at the Consular Section during drop-off hours or send it by tracked post. Processing only starts once all documents are received; missing documents delay processing or cause the file to be returned. Keep the official receipt or reference: you will need it for any status enquiry.

Directions and contact details

By service

What to include for each service

Drawn from the Embassy’s published requirements. Confirm the current list before you submit.

Passport

  • Biometric information sheet from online pre-registration
  • Note de Perception (notice of assessment)
  • Equity BCDC proof of payment
  • Copy of your current or previous Congolese passport (bio page only)
  • At the appointment: the originals, proof of Congolese nationality, a valid U.S. residency document and a prepaid, tracked return envelope

Passport guidance

Visa

  • Application form, completed in full, dated and signed
  • One recent 2 × 2 inch passport photograph
  • Copies of your yellow-fever vaccination card and flight itinerary
  • Original passport valid for six months, with two adjacent blank visa pages
  • Invitation or purpose letter, depending on your category and reason for travel
  • Payment and a prepaid, tracked return envelope

Visa guidance

Tenant‑Lieu (Laissez-Passer)

  • Completed Tenant‑Lieu application form
  • Copy of your current or previous Congolese passport, no more than 6 months past its expiry date, or a written police report if it was lost or stolen
  • Two recent 2 × 2 inch passport photographs
  • Proof of valid U.S. residence, such as a Green Card, study visa or I-20, work permit or employment authorization
  • Prepaid return label or tracked return envelope, and payment by money order, cashier’s check or certified check

Tenant‑Lieu guidance

Civil-status and notarial documents

  • Birth certificate: signed form and evidence of birth in the DRC, such as a passport
  • Marriage certificate: passport copies for both spouses and the marriage record established in the DRC
  • Inheritance certificate: passport copy and the certificate established in the DRC
  • Deportation travel document: the same documents as the Laissez-Passer Tenant-Lieu
  • Repatriation of remains: proof of Congolese citizenship, a certified death certificate, a health authority letter, embalming letter, sealable metal casket and a tracked A4 return envelope
  • Notarial acts and powers of attorney: confirm the originals, identity documents and signatures required before you send anything
