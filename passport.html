Passport services

Use this page to prepare a first application, renewal or replacement request. Passport production and final approval are handled by the competent authorities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Official confirmation required. Requirements, fees, processing expectations and submission methods can change. Last reviewed 4 August 2026 · Content owner: Consular Section.

Who is eligible?

Eligibility

  • Congolese citizens residing in the United States
  • Adults applying for a first issue or renewal
  • Parents or legal guardians applying for a minor
  • Applicants replacing a lost, stolen or damaged passport

What is required?

Documents

  • Printed national passport-portal documents
  • Current or previous passport, when available
  • Proof of Congolese nationality
  • Valid United States residency document
  • Police report for a lost or stolen passport
  • Tracked return envelope and any minor-specific authorizations

What does it cost?

Fee verification

Complete payment only through the officially approved passport process. Confirm the current amount and payment steps on the national passport portal and with the Consular Section before acting.

How long does it take?

Processing guidance

Processing begins only after a complete file and biometric appointment. Production occurs outside the Embassy, so timing can vary. Ask the Consular Section for current expectations.

How do I submit?

Submission steps

  • Obtain the required tax identifier when applicable
  • Complete pre-registration on passeport.gouv.cd
  • Follow the approved payment instructions
  • Send only the requested preparation documents through the approved channel
  • Attend the biometric appointment with original documents

What happens next?

After submission

Keep every official receipt and reference. The Embassy will provide collection or return instructions when the document is ready.

Verified next step

Confirm before you act.

Do not email passport scans, banking details or other sensitive information unless the Embassy provides an approved secure method.
