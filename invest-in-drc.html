Why the DRC

Resources are only the beginning.

The Embassy presents the DRC as a market with extensive farmland, mineral wealth, energy resources and a strategic position in Central Africa. The opportunity is to create more value inside the country through responsible production, infrastructure, services and skilled employment.

Speak with Economic & TradeDiscover the country
ANAPI, the National Agency for Investment Promotion
The Embassy identifies ANAPI as the principal investor-facilitation body. Its support includes orientation, company-establishment guidance, access to investment-code procedures, sector contacts, land or premises searches, partnership development and coordination with relevant authorities.
One-stop supportANAPI is presented as the central facilitation pathway for qualified investors.
10 sectorsThe Embassy publishes dedicated information across ten priority industries.
OHADA memberThe DRC applies the harmonized business-law framework used across participating African states.
Regional accessA continental-scale market connected to Central, East and Southern Africa.

Investor pathway

From inquiry to an informed decision

A disciplined sequence based on the Embassy’s description of ANAPI support and investment-code procedures.

Define the project

Clarify the sector, site, capital, partners, employment plan and the value the project will create inside the DRC.

Engage ANAPI

Request current guidance, confirm the appropriate legal structure and identify sector-specific authorizations.

Prepare the file

Develop the business plan, legal documents, equipment schedule and evidence required for the applicable review.

Verify approvals

Confirm current taxes, incentives, licences, land rights, labour rules and environmental obligations before committing funds.

Published framework

Investment code, protection and incentives

The Embassy’s published guidance describes the following principles. This is an orientation—not legal, tax or financial advice.

Investor support

ANAPI services

  • Company-establishment and administrative orientation
  • Investment-code application guidance
  • Connections to public and private partners
  • Sector licences, land and premises support
Protection

Published safeguards

  • Equal treatment of national and foreign investors
  • Transfer of qualifying investment income abroad
  • Amicable settlement or arbitration pathways
  • DRC participation in OHADA, ICSID and MIGA frameworks
Eligibility

Core conditions listed

  • Operate as an entity under Congolese law
  • Meet the applicable project-cost threshold
  • Comply with environmental and labour rules
  • Demonstrate the required local value-added rate
Tax and customs incentives described by the Embassy

The published summary lists possible exemptions on qualifying machinery, materials and equipment; profits; property tax; and certain company-formation duties for approved projects. It describes benefit periods linked to economic regions. Investors must obtain current, project-specific confirmation from ANAPI and the competent tax and customs authorities.

Investment code and approval procedure

The Embassy describes an ANAPI review followed by consideration through the approval process and an inter-ministerial decision. Required materials may include a project file, equipment list, proof of the company’s legal existence and filing-fee evidence. Confirm the current procedure before preparing or paying for an application.
