Consular case workflow

How a consular file moves through the Embassy, from the moment it is handed in at the counter to the moment it is collected or posted back. The stages, turnaround targets and counter hours below follow the Embassy’s published guidance.

Demonstration

This board is a working model, not the Embassy’s case system. Every file shown is invented for illustration and identified by reference number only. Nothing you do here is saved, sent or recorded — reloading the page restores the original board. The Embassy does not currently offer online case tracking; applications are handed in and collected in person, or sent by post.

Turnaround targets and counter hours

These are the figures the Embassy publishes. The board measures each file against the target for its own service, which is why a passport sitting for three weeks is on time and a diplomatic visa sitting for two days is not.
