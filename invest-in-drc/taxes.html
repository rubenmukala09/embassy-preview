Taxes related to investment in the Democratic Republic of the Congo

The Investment Code provides customs and tax exemptions to investors who submit their investment project (business plan) to ANAPI, the National Agency for Investment Promotion. Once the project is approved, the investor receives the customs, tax and para-fiscal benefits listed below.

Tax and customs benefits

Approved projects receive the following customs, tax and para-fiscal benefits:

  1. Exemption from duties and taxes on imports of machinery, materials and equipment, excluding the 2% administrative fee and VAT. VAT is paid upstream by the promoter and reimbursed by the tax authorities.
  2. Exemption from tax on profits.
  3. Exemption from property tax.
  4. Exemption from the ad valorem duty on the formation of limited liability companies (LLCs) or on increases in their share capital.
The approval decree is delivered within a maximum of 30 days of submitting the application to ANAPI; after this deadline, approval is deemed granted. See the approval procedure.

How long the benefits last

The benefits last 3, 4 or 5 years, depending on the economic region where the investment is located:

Implementing decree of November 1, 2012

Since November 1, 2012, a decree of the Prime Minister on measures implementing the Investment Code has been in force. It reinforces the investor’s benefits by giving approved projects a statutory period of 30 days, on the basis of the file signed by the Managing Director of ANAPI.

To make it easier to carry out the investment, the investor may also, if necessary, present the approval decision issued by ANAPI to the customs administration for the authorized removal of approved equipment, materials and tools.

The legal texts, including Decree No. 12/046 of November 1, 2012, are listed on the page Investment laws.

Requirements to access Investment Code benefits

To access the benefits of the Investment Code, the investor must:

  1. Be an economic entity under Congolese law.
  2. Plan an investment with a total cost (all expenses included) of at least US $200,000, or at least US $10,000 for SMEs and SMIs (small and medium-sized enterprises and industries).
  3. Commit to complying with environmental regulations.
  4. Commit to observing labor regulations.
  5. Ensure a value-added rate of at least 35%.
This page reproduces the Embassy’s published summary. Rates, thresholds and durations can change: confirm the terms that apply to your project with ANAPI and the competent tax (DGI) and customs (DGDA) authorities before investing.
