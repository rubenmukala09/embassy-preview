Laissez-Passer

A Laissez-Passer is a limited temporary document for eligible Congolese citizens who must travel to the Democratic Republic of the Congo without a usable passport. It is not a substitute for a regular passport.

Official confirmation required. Requirements, fees, processing expectations and submission methods can change. Last reviewed 4 August 2026 · Content owner: Consular Section.

Who is eligible?

Eligibility

  • Congolese citizens who need to return to the DRC
  • People whose passport is lost, stolen, expired or unavailable
  • Applicants able to establish Congolese nationality
  • Urgent cases supported by evidence when applicable

What is required?

Documents

  • Completed request form
  • Proof of Congolese nationality
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Police report for a lost or stolen passport
  • Confirmed travel itinerary
  • Supporting evidence for an urgent request

What does it cost?

Fee verification

Confirm the current fee and approved payment method with the Consular Section before submitting. Do not rely on an older online fee list.

How long does it take?

Processing guidance

Timing depends on identity verification, completeness and urgency. Contact the Embassy as early as possible and do not assume same-day issuance.

How do I submit?

Submission steps

  • Call or email the Consular Section first
  • Explain the travel circumstances and proposed date
  • Prepare identity and nationality evidence
  • Follow the approved in-person or mail instructions
  • Keep the official receipt and instructions

What happens next?

After submission

The document is generally limited to the authorized journey. Ask the Consular Section about passport renewal after return.

Verified next step

Confirm before you act.

Do not email passport scans, banking details or other sensitive information unless the Embassy provides an approved secure method.
