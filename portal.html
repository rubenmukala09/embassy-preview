Before you start

What you need
Your completed application form, the original documents with clear photocopies, and valid identification with proof of nationality where your service requires it.
Cost
Set by service and non-refundable. See the published fee schedule.
How to pay
Passports: US $75, paid online through the Equity BCDC link emailed after pre-registration. Other services: blank money order only.
Processing time
Passports take about 90 days, as they are produced in Kinshasa. Visas take from 24 hours to 2 business days, depending on the traveller, once a complete file and payment are received.
Children under 18
A child’s passport application also needs the child’s original birth certificate, a legalized parental authorization and the Congolese parent’s passport.

How to arrange your visit

How you arrange a visit depends on the service.

  • Passports

    By appointment, after online pre-registration

    Obtain your tax identification number (NIF), pre-register on passeport.gouv.cd, pay the US $75 fee through the Equity BCDC link emailed to you, then email your documents to the Embassy. Come in person only once you have received the Embassy’s appointment-confirmation email.

    Passport steps and documents

  • Visas

    At the counter, or by post

    Hand in your complete file during visa drop-off hours, or post it with a prepaid, tracked return envelope. Collect your passport during visa pick-up hours.

    Visa requirements

  • Legalization, Tenant‑Lieu and other services

    Contact the Embassy first

    Requirements differ by document. Confirm what to bring by email or telephone before you visit or post any original.

    Email info@ambadrcusa.org

Service
Child under 18

When to come

Only after the Embassy has emailed your appointment confirmation. Before that, pre-register on passeport.gouv.cd, pay through the Equity BCDC link and email your documents.

What to bring

  • The documents you emailed: biometric information sheet, Note de Perception, proof of payment and passport copy
  • Your current passport, valid, full or expired, or a police report if it was lost or stolen
  • Proof of Congolese nationality
  • A valid U.S. residence document, such as a Green Card, work permit or I‑20
  • A prepaid, tracked return envelope

For the child, also bring

  • The child’s original birth certificate
  • A legalized parental authorization
  • The child’s original Green Card and passport, valid or expired
  • The Congolese parent’s passport

When to come

Visa drop-off: Monday to Thursday 10:00–15:00, Friday 10:00–13:00. You can also send the file by post.

What to bring

  • One application form, completed, dated and signed
  • One recent 2 × 2 inch passport photograph
  • Copies of your yellow-fever vaccination card and flight itinerary
  • Your signed passport, valid for at least six months, with two adjacent blank visa pages
  • The fee by blank money order, and a prepaid, tracked return envelope

For a child under 10, also include

  • The application form completed and signed by a parent
  • A copy of the child’s birth certificate and of the parent’s passport
  • A parental authorization

When to come

Visa pick-up: Tuesday and Thursday 14:00–15:30, Friday 11:00–12:30.

What to bring

  • Photo identification
  • Any receipt or reference the Embassy gave you when you handed in the file

When to come

Contact the Embassy before you visit or post anything: requirements differ by document.

What to bring

  • The completed and signed form
  • A copy of your passport
  • The original document issued in the DRC
  • The fee by blank money order

When to come

Confirm the arrangements with the Embassy first. Office hours: Monday to Thursday 9:00–16:00, Friday 9:00–13:00.

What to bring

  • The completed Tenant-Lieu application form
  • A copy of your current or previous Congolese passport, or a police report if it was lost or stolen
  • Two recent 2 × 2 inch passport photographs
  • Proof of valid U.S. residence
  • A prepaid, tracked return envelope

For a child, contact the Embassy to confirm which additional documents are needed.
