Congo around the world

The DRC Government is committed to assisting visitors and investors with visas, and Congolese nationals living abroad with passports and the Tenant-Lieu. Its embassies, consulates and missions in many countries help make the consular process seamless.

About this directory

Addresses, telephone numbers and email addresses are those published on the Embassy’s previous website (last updated October 21, 2024). Contact details abroad can change: confirm them with the mission concerned before traveling or sending documents.

Missions are grouped by region and listed alphabetically by country. In the United States, consular services for the entire country are provided by the Embassy in Washington, D.C. Consular services
