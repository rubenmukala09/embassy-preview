Visiting the Embassy

The Embassy is in Suite 725 at 1100 Connecticut Avenue NW, an office building in downtown Washington, D.C., near Farragut Square.

Nearest Metro
Farragut North (Red Line) and Farragut West (Blue, Orange and Silver lines), a short walk away.
On arrival
Bring a valid photo ID and your appointment reference, and go to Suite 725.
Parking
Downtown street parking is metered and limited. Public transport is recommended.

Before you contact us

Short answers about appointments, documents and the area the Embassy serves.

For passports, yes: the Embassy confirms your biometric appointment by email once it has received your documents. Follow the passport steps. Visa files are dropped off during the visa drop-off hours. For legalization and other services, contact the Embassy before you travel. There is no online booking.

Requirements differ by document: birth, marriage and inheritance certificates and notarial deeds each need different supporting papers. Read the legalization guidance, and contact the Embassy before you mail irreplaceable originals. Consular fees apply.

Report the loss or theft to your local police and keep a copy of the report. To replace the passport, follow the passport steps. If you must travel before a new passport arrives, apply for a Tenant-Lieu, a temporary document valid for one-way travel to the DRC.

The Embassy in Washington, D.C. provides consular and diplomatic services to Congolese nationals across the entire United States. To confirm the right route for your service, call the consular line.
