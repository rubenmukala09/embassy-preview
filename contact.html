Getting here

Visiting the Embassy

The Embassy is in Suite 725 at 1100 Connecticut Avenue NW, an office building in downtown Washington, D.C., near Farragut Square.

Nearest Metro
Farragut North (Red Line) and Farragut West (Blue, Orange and Silver lines), a short walk away.
On arrival
Bring a valid photo ID and your appointment reference, and go to Suite 725.
Parking
Downtown street parking is metered and limited. Public transport is recommended.

Frequently asked

Before you contact us

Short answers about appointments, documents and the area the Embassy serves.

The pathway depends on the service. Visa files use the published drop-off windows. Passport applicants first complete online pre-registration and payment, then send the requested documents to obtain confirmation for biometric capture. Confirm legalization and other civil-affairs visits directly with the Embassy.
Requirements differ by document: birth, marriage and inheritance certificates and notarial work each need different supporting papers. Read the legalization guidance on the Consular Services page, and contact the Embassy before mailing irreplaceable originals. Consular fees apply.
Report the loss or theft to the police and keep a copy of the report, then contact the Consular Section. If you cannot obtain a replacement passport in time, the Embassy may issue a Tenant-Lieu (laissez-passer), a temporary document valid for one-way travel to the DRC.
The Embassy in Washington, D.C. provides consular and diplomatic services to Congolese nationals across the United States. Contact the Consular Section to confirm the correct channel for your location and service.
