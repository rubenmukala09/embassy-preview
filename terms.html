This is a design preview of an Embassy website. It is not the official website of the Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, its content is not an official act, and nothing here replaces guidance obtained directly from the Consular Section.

2. About this website

The Site gives general information about the Embassy, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the consular services available to the public. It is offered for information only.

No application, appointment or payment is made through this preview. Applications, appointments and fees are handled by the Consular Section through its official channels.

Information about passports, visas, legalization, civil registry and other consular services is provided as guidance. Requirements, fees and processing times change, and every application is subject to the official requirements in force when it is processed.

Nothing on the Site guarantees that a service will be granted, and nothing on it is legal, immigration or financial advice. Confirm current requirements with the Consular Section before you travel, send documents or make a payment.
