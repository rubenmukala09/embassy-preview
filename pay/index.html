Passports are paid online

Obtain your tax number (NIF), pre-register on passeport.gouv.cd, then pay the US $75 fee through the Equity BCDC link sent to you by email. Allow 72 hours for the payment to be recorded.

Step by step

How to pay

For visas, civil-status documents and notarial services.

  1. Find the fee for your service

    Use the schedule of fees on this page. If your service is not listed, ask the Embassy before you pay.

    Go to the schedule of fees

  2. Buy a money order for the exact amount

    Use a blank money order for the exact amount in the schedule of fees, as the Embassy instructs. If you are unsure how to complete it, ask the Embassy before you pay.

  3. Include it with your application

    Hand it in with your file at the Consular Section, or enclose it with a postal application and a prepaid, tracked return envelope.

  4. Keep your receipt

    Keep the official receipt or reference. You will need it for any question about your application.
