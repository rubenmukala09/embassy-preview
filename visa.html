Travel · Entry permission

Visa services

Review the requirements before making travel arrangements. Visa approval is discretionary and depends on a complete application and the rules in force when it is assessed.

Official confirmation required. Requirements, fees, processing expectations and submission methods can change. Last reviewed 4 August 2026 · Content owner: Consular Section.

Who is eligible?

Eligibility

  • United States citizens travelling to the DRC
  • Foreign nationals legally residing in the United States
  • Tourism, business and transit applicants
  • Travellers whose passport remains valid for the required period

What is required?

Documents

  • Completed visa application form
  • Passport with sufficient remaining validity
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Travel itinerary and proof of accommodation
  • Invitation or purpose letter when applicable
  • Yellow-fever vaccination evidence and supporting residency documents

What does it cost?

Fee verification

Visa fees depend on nationality, entry type and validity. Confirm the current fee and accepted payment method with the Consular Section before purchasing a money order.

How long does it take?

Processing guidance

Processing times vary with the visa type, nationality and completeness of the file. Do not purchase non-refundable travel until a visa has been issued.

How do I submit?

Submission steps

  • Choose the correct visa type
  • Prepare the full supporting-document set
  • Confirm the current fee and submission method
  • Submit in person or by the approved mail process
  • Retain the receipt and return-envelope tracking

What happens next?

After submission

The Consular Section may request additional evidence or an interview. Approval is not guaranteed by submission or payment.

Verified next step

Confirm before you act.

Do not email passport scans, banking details or other sensitive information unless the Embassy provides an approved secure method.
