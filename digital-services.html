Proposals

Digital services: what is proposed

Consular services are handled today in person, by post and by email. Three online services are proposed. None is available yet, and each would open only if the Embassy approves it.

  • Proposed, not available

    Appointment requests

    Request a consular appointment online and see the requirements for your service before you come.

    Until then

    Read the appointment guidance

  • Proposed, not available

    Application status

    Check the stage of a passport, visa or legalization file without calling the Embassy.

    Until then

    Email the Consular Section

  • Proposed, not available

    Secure document submission

    Send copies of supporting documents through a secure upload instead of ordinary email.

    Until then

    Follow the published checklists

Not sure where to start?

Contact the Embassy during office hours: Monday to Thursday 9:00–16:00, Friday 9:00–13:00. Send documents only in the way the published guidance for your service describes, and never send banking details by email.
