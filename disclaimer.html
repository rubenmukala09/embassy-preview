In plain language

Information on this preview is drawn from published sources and may be out of date. For anything that affects travel, documents or money, confirm the current position with the Embassy before you act.

1. General information only

The information on this Site is for general information. It is drawn from published sources, may be incomplete or out of date, and is not an official act of the Embassy or of the Government of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

2. No professional advice

Nothing on this Site is legal, immigration, financial, travel or other professional advice. Seek qualified advice, or official guidance from the competent authorities, before acting on it.

3. Visa, travel and immigration information

Information about visas, passports, legalization and entry requirements is guidance and can change without notice. Confirm it with the Consular Section before you book travel or send documents. The granting of any visa, document or service remains at the discretion of the competent authorities.

4. External links

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Those websites are not run by the Embassy, which does not guarantee their accuracy and does not endorse them by linking to them.

5. Liability, symbols and immunities

Liability, the use of official symbols, and the Embassy’s privileges and immunities are covered once, in the Terms & Conditions (sections 5, 7 and 8).

6. Legal notices and takedown requests
