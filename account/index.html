My Embassy

Account support

Online account access is reserved for a later, Embassy-approved service.

A personal Embassy account is planned for a later phase of this website, once the Embassy approves a secure service. Until then, consular requests are handled in person, by post, by telephone or by email.

Prototype service. There is no sign-in on this website. Never enter a password, identity document or payment details here.

Planned account features

Proposed for a future, Embassy-approved service. Features may change before launch.

  • Application status

    Follow a passport, visa or document request using the reference on your official receipt.

  • Appointment notices

    Receive confirmation and collection notices for visits to the Consular Section.

  • Forms and receipts

    Keep copies of the forms you have submitted and the receipts issued by the Embassy.

  • Contact details

    Keep your address, telephone number and email up to date on your consular file.
