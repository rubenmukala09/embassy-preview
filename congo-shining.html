Congo Is Shining

A national showcase of Congolese culture, sport, diaspora leadership and economic opportunity, presented across Houston and Atlanta in 2026.

Host cities

Houston and Atlanta

The campaign connected World Cup match weeks with public diplomacy, community gathering and investment conversations.

Houston, Texas

14–19 June 2026

Published focus: critical minerals, hydropower, green energy, diaspora unity and cultural programming around the NRG Stadium area.

Match: 17 June · DRC v Portugal

Atlanta, Georgia

23–27 June 2026

Published as the second city programme, bringing the cultural event and community gathering to the Mercedes-Benz Stadium area.

Match: 27 June · DRC v Uzbekistan

The campaign stated that cultural-event registration and FIFA match tickets were separate. This archive does not sell or reserve tickets.

Purpose

Three pillars of the programme

The Leopards shine

Sport as a shared language for national pride and global visibility.

The diaspora shines

Congolese communities across the United States gathering, organizing and carrying the national story.

The DRC shines

Culture, creative talent and investment priorities presented together on an international stage.

Programme

Programme themes, 2026

The four themes published for the 2026 programme.

  1. Day 01

    Unity & cohesion

    Diaspora sports, youth-infrastructure discussion and a community gathering.

  2. Day 02

    Faith & partnerships

    Thanksgiving, a strategic U.S.–DRC town hall and Congolese gospel programming.

  3. Day 03

    Banking & finance

    Banking reform, compliance and pathways for diaspora investment.

  4. Day 04

    Mining & energy

    Critical-mineral supply chains and the DRC’s role in the global energy transition.

The Shining Village

Four connected experiences

The campaign described a permanent, multi-day environment rather than a single evening programme.

Fan Zone

Public viewing, music, dance, live media and a consular information hub for Congolese nationals.

Entrepreneurs Expo

A Made in Congo showcase spanning fashion, food, artisan work, cosmetics and trade connections.

Cultural Village

Congolese gastronomy, rumba and ndombolo, art exhibitions and Sapologie fashion programming.

B2B Platform

Investment meetings and panels on mining, energy, banking and Congolese technology innovation.
