The Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo is committed to ensuring its website is accessible to all members of the public.

In plain language

This Embassy preview is designed to be used with a keyboard, a screen reader or a magnifier, and it aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Known limitations are listed on this page, together with a direct route to report a barrier.

1. Conformance status

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive and neurological disabilities.

The Site is currently partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA: most of its content meets the standard, but the items listed in section 4 do not yet. This is a self-assessment made in October 2026 by the team preparing the preview; the Site has not been independently audited.

2. Measures we take to support accessibility

To make this Site accessible, we have implemented and continue to apply the following measures:

Semantic markup
Meaningful HTML structure with headings, landmarks and lists that assistive technologies can interpret.
Keyboard navigation
Interactive elements are operable using a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
Colour contrast
Text and interface colours are chosen to provide sufficient contrast against their backgrounds.
Text alternatives
Informative images are given descriptive alternative text, and decorative images are marked so they are skipped by screen readers.
Scalable text
Content remains legible and usable when text is enlarged or the page is zoomed.
Consistent navigation
Predictable, consistent layout and navigation across pages.

3. Assistive-technology compatibility

This Site is designed to be compatible with recent versions of widely used assistive technologies and browsers, including screen readers and screen magnification software, used on current operating systems. Because technology and browsers evolve, some combinations may produce different results; we welcome reports of any difficulty you experience.

4. Known limitations

The following parts of the Site do not yet fully meet the standard. Each has an alternative:

Application forms (PDF)
The passport, visa and Tenant-Lieu forms are PDF files hosted on the Embassy’s current website and may not work well with screen readers. The Consular Section can help you complete a form or provide it in another format.
Map on the Contact page
The map is embedded from OpenStreetMap and can be hard to use with a keyboard or a screen reader. The Embassy’s full address is given as text on the same page.
Videos
Videos play from YouTube. Captions depend on the original recording and may be missing. The Embassy can provide a written summary on request.

5. Alternative ways to access our services

If any part of this Site is not accessible to you, the Embassy will provide the information or service in another way: by telephone during office hours, by email, or in person at the Consular Section during consular hours. The contact details are set out in section 6.

6. Feedback & reporting accessibility barriers

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this Site. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please let us know. To help us respond effectively, please include the web address (URL) of the page concerned, a description of the problem, and the browser and assistive technology you were using. We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback promptly.

7. If you are not satisfied with our response

You can ask for our response to be reviewed. Escalation uses the same contact details as section 6: write to the Embassy with “Accessibility escalation” in the subject line, or by post for the attention of the Head of Mission.

8. Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is ongoing work. The Site is reviewed as it changes, and this Statement is updated when improvements are made. It was last updated on the date shown at the top of this page.
