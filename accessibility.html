Accessibility Statement

The Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo is committed to ensuring its website is accessible to all members of the public.

The Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo believes that the services of a diplomatic mission should be within reach of everyone. We are committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology.

1. Our commitment to accessibility

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and to apply the relevant accessibility standards, so that all members of the public can access information and services with dignity and independence.

2. Conformance status

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive and neurological disabilities.

The Site is currently partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA, meaning that most, but not all, parts of the content meet the standard. We are actively working to achieve full conformance.

3. Measures we take to support accessibility

To make this Site accessible, we have implemented and continue to apply the following measures:

  • Semantic markup, meaningful HTML structure with headings, landmarks and lists that assistive technologies can interpret.
  • Keyboard navigation, interactive elements are operable using a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Colour contrast, text and interface colours are chosen to provide sufficient contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Text alternatives, informative images are given descriptive alternative text, and decorative images are marked so they are skipped by screen readers.
  • Scalable text, content remains legible and usable when text is enlarged or the page is zoomed.
  • Consistent navigation, predictable, consistent layout and navigation across pages.

4. Assistive-technology compatibility

This Site is designed to be compatible with recent versions of widely used assistive technologies and browsers, including screen readers and screen magnification software, used on current operating systems. Because technology and browsers evolve, some combinations may produce different results; we welcome reports of any difficulty you experience.

5. Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some limitations may remain. We are aware that:

  • certain third-party content or embedded components may not fully meet our accessibility standards;
  • some older documents may not yet be available in a fully accessible format; and
  • maps and other graphical components may have limited text alternatives.

Where you encounter such a limitation, please contact us using the details below and we will provide the information in an alternative format.

6. Alternative ways to access our services

If any part of this Site is not accessible to you, the Embassy will be pleased to assist you through alternative channels:

  • By telephone, call +1 (202) 234-7690 during office hours.
  • By email, write to info@ambadrcusa.org.
  • In person, visit the Consular Section at 1100 Connecticut Avenue NW, Suite 725, Washington, DC 20036 during consular hours.

7. Feedback & reporting accessibility barriers

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this Site. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please let us know. To help us respond effectively, please include the web address (URL) of the page concerned, a description of the problem, and the browser and assistive technology you were using. We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback promptly.

8. Enforcement & contact

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility feedback, you may escalate the matter to the office of the Head of Mission via info@ambadrcusa.org. The Embassy takes accessibility concerns seriously and will make every reasonable effort to resolve them.

9. Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment rather than a one-time effort. We review this Site regularly, incorporate accessibility into our development practices, and update this Statement as improvements are made. This Statement was last reviewed on the date shown above.

Unofficial demo, this website is a demonstration prototype. This Accessibility Statement illustrates the design of a diplomatic mission's website and does not constitute a live legal instrument of any government.
