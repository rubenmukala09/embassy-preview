Before you follow a link

  • These websites are run by their own institutions, not by the Embassy.
  • Before you sign in or pay, check that the address bar shows the domain listed here.
  • Never send a password, card number or identity document by email.
  • Ignore look-alike addresses in adverts or unsolicited messages. If a link does not open, use the ministry’s main website or contact the Embassy.
