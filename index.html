The Embassy

The Head of State and the Ambassador

The Embassy advances the interests of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and its citizens, and strengthens cooperation, peace, prosperity and sustainable development with the United States.

Head of State

H.E. Félix-Antoine Tshisekedi Tshilombo

President of the Democratic Republic of the Congo

The Embassy carries out its mission under the leadership of the President of the Republic.

Head of Mission

H.E. Yvette Kapinga Ngandu

Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary

A career diplomat, she most recently served as Commissioner for Gender Promotion, Human and Social Development at the Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS). At the African Union, she helped establish the Continental Mediation Unit and the Secretariat of the Panel of the Wise.

Travel and registration

Travelling to or living in the DRC?

Check the entry requirements before you travel, and register to receive Embassy safety and consular notices.

Before you travel

Visa
Required for most travellers.
Passport
Valid for at least six months, with two blank pages.
Health
A yellow-fever vaccination certificate is mandatory.
Supporting documents
Requirements vary for tourism, business, study and official travel.

Also consult current government travel, security and health guidance for every destination on your itinerary.

Official release·May 11, 2026

The Washington Agreements: peace, sovereignty and prosperity for the DRC(opens the Embassy website in a new tab)

The Embassy describes the Washington Agreements as a three-part framework: the DRC–Rwanda Peace Agreement, a Regional Economic Integration Framework and a strategic partnership between the DRC and the United States.

Consular guidance

Two-year reciprocity visa for U.S. citizens

The Embassy publishes a two-year US$185 reciprocity visa for U.S. citizens travelling for tourism, study or business. The supporting documents for the purpose of travel are still required.

Passport services

Biometric passport data capture at the Embassy

Data capture for the biometric Congolese passport is available at the Embassy. Complete the online pre-registration and payment first, then attend your appointment with your original documents.
