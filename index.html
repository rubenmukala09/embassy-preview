Welcome

Information for visitors, investors and the Congolese diaspora

Welcome to the official website of the Embassy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) in the United States. We extend a warm invitation to explore all the essential information for your visit to our beautiful country. The DRC boasts the world’s second-largest river basin and is home to five natural UNESCO World Heritage sites: four national parks and the Okapi Wildlife Reserve. Whether you are a tourist seeking adventure or an investor looking to engage with the DRC, we believe your visit will contribute significantly to our country’s vision.

On this website, you will find essential information on consular services for tourists, investors and the Congolese diaspora. You can also stay updated on the DRC’s development initiatives and access key resources, including official government statements.

  • Visas, passports and travel documents

    Visas for all travelers, including foreign applicants; passports and travel documents for Congolese nationals; legalization of documents and powers of attorney.Application information

  • Civil affairs for the Congolese diaspora

    The DRC Government is committed to facilitating travel for Congolese nationals living abroad. The Embassy assists with DRC passport issuance and renewal and with the Tenant-Lieu.We are here to serve you

  • The Embassy

    The Embassy represents the interests of the DRC in the United States and provides diplomatic engagement, consular services and support for Congolese nationals, visitors, investors and the diaspora.About the Embassy

  • DRC representations worldwide

    The DRC is represented in many countries to help visitors and investors with visas, and Congolese nationals living abroad with passports and the Tenant-Lieu.Find out where we are

The Embassy

The Head of State and the Ambassador

The Embassy advances the interests of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and its citizens, and strengthens cooperation, peace, prosperity and sustainable development with the United States.

Head of State

H.E. Félix-Antoine Tshisekedi Tshilombo

President of the Democratic Republic of the Congo

In office since January 24, 2019. The Embassy carries out its mission under the authority of the President of the Republic.

Head of Mission

H.E. Yvette Kapinga Ngandu

Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary

Appointed in June 2025, she took up her post in Washington on October 30, 2025. A career diplomat, she most recently served as Commissioner for Gender Promotion, Human and Social Development at the Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS).

Travel advice

Traveling to or living in the DRC?

Check the entry requirements before you travel, and register to receive Embassy safety and consular notices.

Before you travel

Visa
Required for most travelers.
Passport
Valid for at least six months, with two blank pages.
Health
A yellow-fever vaccination certificate is mandatory.
Supporting documents
Requirements vary for tourism, business, study and official travel.

Also consult current government travel, security and health guidance for every destination on your itinerary.

Invest in the DRC

The Democratic Republic of the Congo boasts some of the most abundant natural resources on the planet. With a strong economy, rich farmland and bountiful mineral wealth, the DRC is progressively building towards a brighter future. ANAPI is mandated to simplify the investment process, make procedures more transparent, assist foreign investors and improve the image of the DRC as an investment destination.
